# Viral Short Video Radar

Use this skill to produce a daily short-video inspiration digest for a Malaysia-based creative designer who sells AI video, product promo, and social media creative services to SMEs, creators, ecommerce sellers, FMCG businesses, and local business owners.

## Default Output

Return a Markdown digest in Chinese by default, with English or Malay phrases only when useful for Malaysia-local hooks.

Start with:

```markdown
# Today's 4 Short-Video Inspirations
```

Then provide exactly 4 briefs unless public-source availability is limited. Each brief must include:

- Source link and platform/source type
- Category: AIGC product promo, educational, comedy/abstract, AI story/short drama, news/festival, or high-signal wildcard
- Why it is worth studying
- Hook idea
- Visual/structure breakdown
- Malaysia-local adaptation for SME, creator, ecommerce, or FMCG audience
- Seedance / AI video production direction
- Suggested platform: Instagram, TikTok, or both
- Suggested CTA for DM/WhatsApp consultation

End with:

```markdown
## Today's Best Bet
```

Recommend the one idea most likely to generate inquiries.

## Source Policy

Use compliant public sources only:

- Public web/news/trend pages
- TikTok Creative Center and other official/public trend resources
- Accessible public Instagram/TikTok/Reels/Xiaohongshu/Douyin pages
- User-provided account lists, links, hashtags, screenshots, or saved references
- Public holiday/event calendars and Malaysia-relevant news sources

Do not bypass logins, solve access restrictions, scrape private content, automate unauthorized platform actions, download/repost videos, or claim private platform metrics as verified. If a source shows only public qualitative signals, say so. Use direct links and concise paraphrases, not full copied captions, transcripts, or scripts.

If a platform blocks access or data is not verifiable, use a public fallback source and include a short caveat.

## Candidate Collection

Collect more candidates than needed, then select the strongest 4. Prioritize recency when the user asks for today/daily/latest.

Use searches and source checks around:

- AI product video, AIGC ad, AI commercial, product promo, product launch video
- TikTok Creative Center trends, trending ads, trending hashtags, trending songs
- Instagram Reels product video, creator marketing, ecommerce video ideas
- Malaysia SME, Malaysia ecommerce, Malaysia F&B, Malaysia FMCG, local business marketing
- TikTok Malaysia trends, Raya, Merdeka, CNY, Deepavali, Christmas, payday, school holiday, mega sale
- Xiaohongshu/RedNote product seeding ideas, Douyin product ad ideas, AI story shorts

When possible, balance the final 4 across:

1. AIGC product promo or portfolio-worthy visual idea
2. Educational/knowledge content for TikTok authority
3. Comedy, entertainment, or abstract engagement format
4. News/festival/AI story/high-signal wildcard

## Scoring

Score candidates mentally before selecting. Favor ideas with:

- Freshness: current or seasonally relevant
- Virality signal: public trend page ranking, repeated format, visible engagement, news momentum, or cross-platform repetition
- Malaysia business relevance: useful for local SMEs, creators, ecommerce sellers, FMCG, F&B, beauty, service businesses, or retail
- Creative replicability: can be adapted without copying the original
- AI-video fit: suitable for Seedance or other AIGC workflows
- Conversion potential: naturally supports DM/WhatsApp inquiry, portfolio trust, or service education

Avoid ideas that are viral but hard to connect to the user's services.

## Briefing Style

Write like a practical creative strategist, not a trend list. The user should be able to start scripting or producing immediately.

For each idea:

- Explain the creative mechanism, not just the topic.
- Localize to Malaysia with language, culture, purchasing moments, or business categories.
- Include a concrete content angle for either Instagram portfolio trust or TikTok awareness.
- Make the CTA low-friction and consultation-oriented.

Do not tell the user to copy the original video. Frame every idea as adaptation, remix, or structure study.

## Caveats

If fewer than 4 high-quality public examples are found, still return the best available public-source ideas and mark any weaker item as a fallback. Never fabricate sources, metrics, screenshots, or platform rankings.
