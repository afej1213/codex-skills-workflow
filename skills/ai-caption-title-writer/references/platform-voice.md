# Platform Voice Guide

Read this file whenever generating the formal Caption or any platform variation.

## Shared Malaysia-local tone

- Write primarily in the user's language; default to Simplified Chinese when the input is Chinese.
- Sound friendly, commercially aware, grounded, and lightly humorous.
- Use Manglish only as seasoning: `boss`, `lah`, `steady`, `actually`, `can ah`, `PM me`.
- Do not stack Manglish expressions or make the copy harder to understand.
- Keep paragraphs short for mobile reading and use Emoji sparingly.
- Preserve the user's verified facts. Never invent prices, specifications, colors, locations, stock, certifications, awards, results, or customer claims.

## General / Instagram formal version

- Balance brand polish, useful information, emotion, and interaction.
- Open with one pain point, contrast, question, concrete benefit, or relatable scene.
- Explain why the product or service matters instead of listing features without context.
- Close with one primary action.

## Xiaohongshu version

- Sound like a real recommendation, observation, or question rather than a vendor pitch.
- Prefer a light question or rhetorical hook and soft-sell language.
- When the user names sensitive or overly direct transaction terms, avoid those exact terms and use context-appropriate alternatives such as `了解细节`, `交流需求`, `收藏参考`, or `想知道更多`.
- Never claim that word substitution guarantees traffic or prevents throttling.
- Keep search-friendly hashtags relevant to the actual category and Malaysia context.

## TikTok version

- Put the strongest contrast, pain point, or benefit in the first two sentences.
- Use short lines and spoken rhythm suitable for a video Caption or voiceover.
- Allow slightly more Manglish than other versions, but keep it natural.
- End with one low-friction action such as a short comment prompt or DM keyword.

## Facebook boss-audience version

- Emphasize practical value, trust, operating context, and decision usefulness.
- Use a mature, clear tone with fewer decorative phrases.
- For consumer products, stay consumer-relevant instead of forcing procurement or business-owner pain points.
- Use a consultation CTA only when it fits the content and funnel stage.

## Adaptation rule

Make each platform version materially different in hook, rhythm, emphasis, and CTA. Do not merely replace the platform name or shuffle identical sentences.
